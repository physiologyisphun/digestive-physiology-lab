Critical-thinking questions

Why does bile help fat digestion without being an enzyme? What happens to glucose after it reaches the liver? Why do most long-chain dietary fats first enter lymph?

B. Hormone Control Room

You're running the duodenal and gastric control panels. For each alert, choose the primary hormone that best fits the situation. The feedback explains the physiological response.

Combined signal challenge

Acidic, fatty chyme enters the duodenum. Which two hormones respond? Which pancreatic cell types respond, and what do they secrete?

C. GI Motility Mystery

The animations are schematic movement cues, not footage of human intestines. Watch each and diagnose the pattern. Identify the primary function as well.

Case investigation

What distinguishes gastric retropulsion from acid reflux? Why can't segmentation replace peristalsis?

D. Colorimetric Crime Scene

Four food samples have lost their labels. Interpret the controls first. Then identify which unknown is consistent with starch, reducing sugar, protein, or an oil emulsion. No household testing is needed.
